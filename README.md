# Healing
Healing KB
AI-Powered Emotional, Mental & Physical Wellness Knowledge Base
1. Project Overview

Healing KB is an intelligent wellness knowledge platform designed to identify relationships between emotional trauma, mental health challenges, and physical health conditions. The system combines structured medical/wellness information with AI-assisted interpretation to help users better understand underlying emotional patterns and healing pathways.

The platform organizes conditions into categorized knowledge modules and provides guided insights, trauma associations, therapeutic recommendations, and progress tracking tools.

2. Problem Statement

Many individuals experience emotional, mental, and physical symptoms without understanding the deeper causes or connections between them. Traditional systems often separate physical health from emotional wellbeing, making it difficult for users to identify holistic healing approaches.

Current challenges include:

Lack of centralized wellness knowledge systems
Difficulty identifying emotional root causes
Limited personalized healing guidance
Fragmented mental and physical health resources
Poor long-term progress tracking
